What is i-HACK?

i-Hack are a series of hackathons held during IIT Bombay's E-Summit, organized by E-Cell, which bring together aspiring product managers, strategists, growth specialists, developers, designers, innovators and future entrepreneurs from across India. This hackathon fosters collaboration within the developer community, offering a platform to create innovative, real-world solutions in a high-energy environment.
